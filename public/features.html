Application Features

Bantha Tracker is built to help collectors organize inventory, record what they actually paid, and understand their cost basis before they buy, trade, or resell.

👤

Private User Accounts

Create an account and sign in securely. Each user’s inventory and purchase history is kept separate from every other account.

▤

Collection & Inventory Tracking

Add figures to your personal inventory and keep a description for variant, condition, completeness, grading, or other collector-specific details.

$

Recommended Buy Targets

Store a recommended buy price for each figure so you can quickly compare an asking price against the number you want to pay.

↗

Recommended Retail Targets

Save a retail target beside each item to help guide resale pricing and evaluate potential margin before making a purchase.

＋

Record Multiple Purchases

Record purchases against a figure over time, including quantity, base purchase price, tax, and shipping.

Σ

Total Invested

Bantha Tracker totals the money invested in each figure so you can see how much capital is tied up in that inventory.

Ø

Average Landed Cost

Your average landed cost is calculated from purchase price plus applicable purchase costs, helping you know your real per-unit cost.

#

Quantity Owned

Track how many units you have purchased for each figure and see the owned quantity directly from your inventory view.

✎

Variant & Condition Notes

Use item descriptions to distinguish variants, accessories, condition, grading details, packaging differences, or anything else that matters to the collectible.

◆

Collector-Focused Workflow

The app is designed around a simple collector workflow: identify the item, know your buy target, record the purchase, track your cost, and plan the resale.
